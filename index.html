Surge Shorts Uploader

A personal, single-owner automation tool run by its developer, Taegyun Kim. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up.

What it does

The tool creates short market-news videos and uploads them privately to Taegyun Kim’s own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It also writes production logs to Google Sheet and Drive files in that same developer’s own Google account.

Google Cloud project: surge-shorts.

Who it is for

Only the developer. There is no public product, no account creation, and no access for other people. The app does not collect data from anyone else.

Google services it uses

  • YouTube Data API, to upload private videos, captions, and thumbnails to the developer’s own channel.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets, to record production logs in files the app creates in the developer’s own Drive.

How that data is used, stored, and deleted is described in the Privacy Policy.
